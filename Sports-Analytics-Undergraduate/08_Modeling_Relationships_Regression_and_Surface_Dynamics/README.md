# Module 08: Modeling Relationships: Regression and Surface Area Dynamics

**Maps to:** Chapter 5, Modelling Relationships of the course textbook (Zuccolotto, P., & Manisera, M. (2020). *Basketball Data Science: With Applications in R*. Chapman & Hall/CRC Data Science Series, CRC Press.)

**Throughline(s):** Positioning and shifting; game analysis

## Overview

Real simple linear and nonparametric regression, real mean-variability
"surface area" bubble diagrams, and a real correlation network,
applied to the 2017-18 NBA season to model how basketball variables
actually relate to one another, from team Net Rating predicting wins
down to individual shooting-percentage variability.
