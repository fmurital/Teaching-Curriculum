# Module 10: Quality Monitoring and Injury Risk (Signature Module)

**Maps to:** Not from the course textbook -- built from the instructor's own published and under-review research of the course textbook (Zuccolotto, P., & Manisera, M. (2020). *Basketball Data Science: With Applications in R*. Chapman & Hall/CRC Data Science Series, CRC Press.)

**Throughline(s):** Injury detection & prediction

## Overview

Control charts and process-capability (Cp) methods applied to athlete workload and injury data, built from Faruk Muritala's own published methodology (Muritala, Brown & Haller, 2026, *Research in Sports Medicine*, on MLB pitcher reinjury) and his NBA reinjury manuscript currently under review at *Basketball Studies*. The module is taught in explicit contrast with the acute:chronic workload ratio (ACWR) literature commonly used in sports science, alongside a paired paper on statistical process control for sport-science data quality (West et al., 2026, *Journal of Strength and Conditioning Research*) and documented critiques of the ACWR approach (Kanwal et al., 2025). This module is also the course's direct bridge to graduate, master's, and PhD level final-project work, where students can extend the instructor's own methodology.
