# Quality Monitoring & Process Control in Sports (signature module)

**Scheduled:** Weeks 10-11 (see `00_Course_Admin/Course_Blueprint.md` for the full weekly schedule)
**Level:** Undergraduate

## Overview

Control charts and process capability (Cp) applied to athlete performance/injury data, built from Faruk Muritala's own published methodology (Muritala, Brown & Haller, 2026, Research in Sports Medicine) and the parallel West et al. (2026, JSCR) paper, taught in explicit contrast with the acute:chronic workload ratio literature and its documented statistical weaknesses (Kanwal et al., 2025, and related critiques).

Depth: practical application, visualization, and communication outcomes.
