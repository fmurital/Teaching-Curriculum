# Module 4 Videos

- **"NBA Shot Charts: A Basketball Data Visualization"** (YouTube:
  https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CBKQNdGYGCc). A concept-explainer
  video showing how shot chart data visualizations are built and read,
  giving students a visual introduction to shot chart analytics before
  we build our own from real play-by-play data. Exact channel name and
  runtime were not confirmed at the time this was added; verify both
  before assigning, and swap in an alternative if the video has since
  been removed or re-titled.

- **"Stephen Curry Full Game 1 Highlights vs Cavaliers 2018 NBA
  Finals - 29 Pts, 9 Ast, CLUTCH!"** (YouTube:
  https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vnVpvrywH80). Real game footage from
  the same 2018 postseason this module's data comes from, useful for
  showing students what a real three-point-heavy shot profile looks
  like in action on the floor. Exact channel name and runtime were not
  confirmed at the time this was added; verify both before assigning.

No coding-syntax tutorial video is assigned for this module; the
primary worked example is the live, real-data walkthrough in
`Coding_Exercise/module4_shot_charts.R` and Lab 4.
