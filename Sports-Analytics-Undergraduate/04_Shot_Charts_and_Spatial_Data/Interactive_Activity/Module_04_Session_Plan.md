# Module 4 Session Plan: Shot Charts and Spatial Data

**Format:** one 3-hour class session, three segments, two 10-minute breaks.

## Segment 1 (50 minutes): Coordinates and the first real shot chart

- I'll recap where Module 3 left off and introduce today's new variable:
  location (5 min).
- I'll walk through why raw shot coordinates need to be transformed
  before we can chart them, live in RStudio, building the half-court
  centered coordinates together (15 min).
- We'll build our first real shot chart together, Houston's 273 real
  2018 playoff attempts, and look at where the makes and misses cluster
  (20 min).
- I want you to turn to a partner and describe, out loud, what you see
  in the chart before I reveal the zone breakdown (10 min).

**Break (10 minutes).**

## Segment 2 (50 minutes): Zones, Moreyball, and two real teams

- I'll introduce the four distance zones and we'll compute the real
  leaguewide zone table together, live (15 min).
- Short lecture: I'll walk through the real Houston-versus-Cleveland
  zone comparison and what "Moreyball" actually means as a real,
  data-driven shot-selection strategy, not just a nickname (10 min).
- Small groups (3-4 students): each group gets one more real playoff
  team from `PbP.BDB` and builds that team's own zone table, then
  reports whether it looks more like Houston's or Cleveland's profile
  (20 min).
- Groups report out (5 min).

**Break (10 minutes).**

## Segment 3 (50 minutes): Two real players and honest reading

- We'll build Harden's and LeBron's real shot charts together and
  compare their zone tables side by side (20 min).
- I'll walk through the "reading a shot chart honestly" section of the
  notes: why a visually striking cluster needs a real attempt count
  behind it before you trust it (10 min).
- Discussion: I'll ask the class what a scout or a front office would
  actually do differently after seeing a player's real shot chart,
  versus just their season shooting percentage (10 min).
- I'll assign Lab 4, the practice questions, and the Module 4 quiz, and
  preview Module 5 (correlation and player networks) (10 min).
