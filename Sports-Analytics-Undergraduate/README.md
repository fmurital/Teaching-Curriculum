# Sports Analytics (Undergraduate)

Course number, credit hours, and term are still being finalized.

This is the **Undergraduate** version of the Sports Analytics course. It's a self-contained repository: everything needed to teach, learn, assess, and improve the course lives here, and it doesn't depend on the companion `Sports-Analytics-Graduate` folder or on anything outside this one.

**Textbook:** Zuccolotto, P., & Manisera, M. (2020). *Basketball Data Science: With Applications in R*. Chapman & Hall/CRC Data Science Series, CRC Press.

## Modules

1. [Introduction to Basketball Analytics](01_Introduction_to_Basketball_Analytics/)
2. [Pace, Ratings, and the Four Factors](02_Pace_Ratings_and_the_Four_Factors/)
3. [Visualizing Team and Player Profiles](03_Visualizing_Team_and_Player_Profiles/)
4. [Shot Charts and Spatial Data](04_Shot_Charts_and_Spatial_Data/)
5. [Discovering Patterns: Correlation and Player Networks](05_Discovering_Patterns_Correlation_and_Player_Networks/)
6. [Event Density and Shooting Under Pressure](06_Event_Density_and_Shooting_Under_Pressure/)
7. [Finding Groups: Clustering Players and Teams](07_Finding_Groups_Clustering_Players_and_Teams/)
8. [Modeling Relationships: Regression and Surface Area Dynamics](08_Modeling_Relationships_Regression_and_Surface_Dynamics/)
9. [The BasketballAnalyzeR Package: Applied Toolkit](09_The_BasketballAnalyzeR_Package_Applied_Toolkit/)
10. [Quality Monitoring and Injury Risk (Signature Module)](10_Quality_Monitoring_and_Injury_Risk_Signature_Module/)

Modules map directly onto the course textbook's chapters, in order, with one signature module (10) built from the instructor's own research rather than the textbook.

Two topics from the course's original ten-topic draft were dropped from the numbered module sequence during the September 2026 restructure: **Data Collection & Sports Data Sources** and **Communication & Ethics**. Neither maps to a chapter in the confirmed course textbook, and per this course's evidence policy (verifiable sports-analytics sources only), generic content for them was not invented to fill the slot. Either could return later as a real module if a specific, verified textbook or paper is chosen to anchor it.

**Salary Structure Analysis** is one of the course's five thematic throughlines but currently has no confirmed textbook chapter or verified literature source. It is not built into any module until a specific source is confirmed, or the throughline is formally dropped.

## Status

Modules are going up one at a time; each module's own README says whether it's built yet. Module 1 is being built first, in full (slides, instructor and student notes, lab, coding exercise, practice questions, autograded assignment, solutions, references, and a short quiz), before later modules get the same treatment.
