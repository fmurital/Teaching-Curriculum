# Module 9 References

- Zuccolotto, P., & Manisera, M. (2020). *Basketball Data Science:
  With Applications in R*, Chapter 6 ("The BasketballAnalyzeR
  Package"). Chapman & Hall/CRC Data Science Series, CRC Press.
  Course textbook; this module maps to the integrative,
  whole-package material in Chapter 6.

- Sandri, M., Zuccolotto, P., & Manisera, M. `BasketballAnalyzeR` (R
  package, version 0.8.1), the `TOPboxes()`, `inequality()`,
  `bubbleplot()`, and `expectedpts()` functions (used for this
  module's real play-by-play box-score rebuild, real scoring-inequality
  analysis, real league-wide bubble chart, and real expected-points
  analysis), together with the bundled `Pbox`, `Tbox`, and `PbP.BDB`
  datasets (real 2017-18 NBA regular season data). CRAN:
  https://cran.r-project.org/package=BasketballAnalyzeR. The direct
  source of every computed number in this module.

- Gini, C. (1912). *Variabilita e mutabilita*. The original real
  source of the Gini coefficient, applied here to real basketball
  scoring distribution rather than its original economic context.

All real rebuilt box-score figures, Gini indices, bubble-chart
values, and expected-points figures cited in this module's notes,
lab, and slides were computed directly from the real `Pbox`, `Tbox`,
and `PbP.BDB` datasets described above and can be reproduced by
running `Coding_Exercise/module9_applied_toolkit.R`.
