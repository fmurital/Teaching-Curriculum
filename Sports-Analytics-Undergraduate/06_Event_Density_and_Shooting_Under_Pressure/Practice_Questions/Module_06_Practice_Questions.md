# Module 6 Practice Questions (ungraded)

1. In your own words, explain what an event density plot shows that a
   simple average (like "the average shot happens at 11 seconds into
   the shot clock") would not show.

2. This module uses `playlength`, the real number of seconds elapsed
   in the current possession when an event was logged, as its measure
   of shot-clock pressure. Why does a higher `playlength` value mean
   more pressure on the shooter, not less?

3. The real shot-clock buckets in this module show field goal
   percentage falling from 49.6% (early clock) to 47.0% (mid clock) to
   42.4% (late clock). Is a 7.2 percentage point drop, on its own,
   proof that pressure causes worse shooting, or could something else
   about late-clock shots explain part of the drop? Explain your
   reasoning.

4. Three-point shooting in this module is actually worst in the
   earliest shot-clock bucket and the latest shot-clock bucket, and
   best in the middle. Propose one real basketball explanation for why
   early three-point attempts might also be lower percentage shots.

5. This module defines "clutch time" as the last 5 minutes of the
   fourth period or overtime with the score within 5 points. Why might
   a stricter definition (say, the last 2 minutes, margin within 3)
   give you a less reliable percentage, even if it feels like a
   "purer" measure of pressure?

6. The real clutch-time field goal percentage in this module (41.7%)
   is lower than the real non-clutch field goal percentage (47.5%).
   Name two different real explanations for that gap: one about the
   shot itself, and one about who is taking it.

7. Kevin Durant had the most real clutch field goal attempts in this
   module's data (42), but his real clutch field goal percentage
   (47.6%) was still lower than a typical non-clutch NBA field goal
   percentage. Does that make him a bad clutch shooter? What else
   would you want to know before answering?

8. This module's data comes from `PbP.BDB`, which is built around
   Golden State's full 82-game 2017-18 schedule. Why does that mean
   the clutch-time player table is dominated by Warriors and the
   specific opponents they played close games against, rather than
   being a league-wide clutch leaderboard?
