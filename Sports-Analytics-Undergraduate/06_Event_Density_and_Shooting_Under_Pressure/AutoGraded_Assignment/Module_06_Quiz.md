# Module 6 Quiz (Microsoft Forms, 8 questions, multiple choice)

Paste each question into forms.office.com as its own Choice question; mark the correct option using Forms' own settings.

**Q1.** `BasketballAnalyzeR::densityplot()` estimates a smoothed curve
showing:
A. The average value of a variable across all players
B. Where events (like shot attempts) cluster along a time variable,
   such as seconds elapsed in the shot clock
C. The correlation between two box score stats
D. A team's win total over a season
**Correct answer: B**

**Q2.** This module uses `playlength` as its shot-clock pressure
variable. What does `playlength` actually measure?
A. The total length of the game in minutes
B. The real number of seconds elapsed in the current possession when
   an event was logged
C. A player's total minutes played that season
D. The distance of a shot from the basket
**Correct answer: B**

**Q3.** In this module's real data, field goal percentage falls from
49.6% (under 12 seconds elapsed) to 42.4% (18+ seconds elapsed). What
is the size of that real drop?
A. About 1 percentage point
B. About 7 percentage points
C. About 20 percentage points
D. There is no real drop; the percentages are equal
**Correct answer: B**

**Q4.** In this module's real three-point shooting breakdown by
shot-clock bucket, which bucket has the highest real 3P%?
A. Early clock (under 12s elapsed)
B. Mid clock (12-17s elapsed)
C. Late clock, high pressure (18s+ elapsed)
D. All three buckets have identical 3P%
**Correct answer: B**

**Q5.** This module defines real "clutch time" using the same
definition NBA.com's own advanced stats use. What is that definition?
A. The entire fourth quarter, any score margin
B. The last 5 minutes of the fourth period or overtime, with the
   score within 5 points
C. Any shot taken in overtime only
D. The last 2 minutes of any period, any score margin
**Correct answer: B**

**Q6.** In this module's real data, clutch-time field goal percentage
(41.7%) compares to non-clutch field goal percentage (47.5%) how?
A. Clutch shooting is higher than non-clutch shooting
B. Clutch shooting is lower than non-clutch shooting
C. They are exactly equal
D. Clutch time has no field goal attempts in this dataset
**Correct answer: B**

**Q7.** Kevin Durant led all players in this module's real clutch
field goal attempts (42), but his real clutch field goal percentage
(47.6%) was still lower than many players' overall season shooting
percentage. What does this module say is the right way to read that?
A. It proves Durant is a poor clutch shooter with no further
   explanation needed
B. Clutch shots are, on average, harder shots against tougher
   defense, so a lower percentage on real clutch attempts does not by
   itself prove worse shot-making
C. The number must be a data error, since star players always shoot a
   higher percentage in the clutch
D. Clutch attempts and shooting percentage cannot be compared to each
   other at all
**Correct answer: B**

**Q8.** Why is the real player-level clutch-attempts table in this
module dominated by Golden State Warriors players and the specific
opponents they played close games against?
A. `PbP.BDB` only contains data for the Golden State Warriors and no
   other team
B. `PbP.BDB` is built around Golden State's full 82-game 2017-18
   schedule, so other teams appear only in their own real games
   against Golden State
C. Clutch time only happens in games involving the Warriors
D. The dataset excludes all non-Warriors players entirely
**Correct answer: B**
