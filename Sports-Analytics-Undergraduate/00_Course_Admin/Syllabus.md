## Course Description

This course introduces the field of sports analytics through basketball, using real NBA data and the R programming language. Students learn to describe team and player performance statistically, discover patterns in game data, group players and teams by statistical profile, and model relationships between performance variables, building toward an applied analysis of a team or player of their choosing. The course closes with a module on quality-monitoring methods for athlete workload and injury risk.

## Course Learning Outcomes

- Explain what sports analytics is and how it is used by teams, media, and researchers.
- Compute and interpret pace, offensive/defensive ratings, and the Four Factors for a team.
- Build and read shot charts and other spatial representations of on-court data.
- Use correlation and network methods to describe relationships between players and performance variables.
- Apply k-means and hierarchical clustering to group players and teams by statistical profile.
- Fit and interpret linear and nonparametric regression models on basketball data.
- Use the `BasketballAnalyzeR` R package as an integrated toolkit across the methods above.
- Apply control-chart and process-capability methods to athlete workload and injury data.
- Communicate a data analysis clearly in written and presented form.
