# Module 1 Videos

## MIT Sloan Sports Analytics Conference

Creator/Source: MIT Sloan Sports Analytics Conference (official conference channel and site)
Where to find it: https://www.sloansportsconference.com/content and its YouTube channel
Learning Objective: Give students a real sense of the professional sports analytics field this course is preparing them for.
Placement in Lecture: Segment A, as an optional preview linked from D2L rather than played live in class (duration varies by panel and is not listed on the source page).
Discussion Questions: What kind of question is this panel trying to answer? Is it closer to description or to prediction? Which of the course's five throughlines, if any, does it touch on?

Example real panel title from the conference's own content page: "How the NBA has evolved data strategy to serve the broadening definition of membership." Duration is not listed on the source page.

No additional video content is assigned for Module 1. Later modules will link specific, verified conference talks or demonstrations where they are a genuine fit for that module's content, rather than added here for their own sake.
