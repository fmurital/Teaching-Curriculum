# Module 3 Practice Questions (Ungraded)

These are for your own practice and do not count toward your grade. Try
each one before checking your work in RStudio.

1. In your own words, what problem does standardizing a variable (a
   z-score) solve that a raw table of per-36-minute stats does not?

2. Write out the z-score formula and explain, in one sentence, what a
   z-score of -1.5 means about a value relative to its group.

3. A player has a three-point rate z-score of +1.3 and a rebound rate
   z-score of -0.9 within a five-player comparison group. Describe that
   player's role in one or two sentences, without using the words
   "good" or "bad."

4. Name one specific criticism professional NBA analysts have raised
   about radar/radial charts, and explain why checking the underlying
   z-score table protects against that criticism.

5. `barline()` stacks several categorical variables as bars and overlays
   a continuous variable as a line on a second axis. Why is playing time
   (minutes) a reasonable choice for the line variable when comparing a
   team's shooting percentages?

6. Coefficient of variation is defined as CV = sd(x) / mean(x). Two
   players have the same standard deviation in their game-to-game
   scoring, but one player averages 10 points a game and the other
   averages 25. Which player has the higher CV, and what does that mean
   about their relative consistency?

7. In the 2017-18 Houston Rockets data, weighted free-throw percentage
   had a higher coefficient of variation (about 16.2%) than weighted
   three-point percentage (about 6.6%). Propose one basketball reason
   this might be true, beyond just "small sample size."

8. Assists per game correlate with wins at about 0.25 across the
   2017-18 season, far weaker than net rating's 0.96 correlation from
   Module 2. Does this mean passing does not matter for winning? Explain
   why or why not.
