# Module 3 References

- Zuccolotto, P., & Manisera, M. (2020). *Basketball Data Science: With
  Applications in R*, Chapter 2 ("Data and Basic Statistical Analyses").
  Chapman & Hall/CRC Data Science Series, CRC Press. Course textbook;
  this module maps to the second half of Chapter 2, on comparison plots
  and visualizing team and player profiles.

- Zuccolotto, P., & Manisera, M. `BasketballAnalyzeR` (R package). The
  `radialprofile()`, `barline()`, `variability()`, and `scatterplot()`
  functions and their documentation are the direct source of this
  module's profile visualizations, run on the package's bundled real
  2017-18 NBA season data (`Pbox`, `Tbox`).

- Whitehead, T. (2017, May 19). "Nylon Calculus: Bravely staring down
  the radar chart backlash." *FanSided*.
  https://fansided.com/2017/05/19/nylon-calculus-defense-radar-charts/.
  Discusses NBA analytics leaders' criticism of radar charts (including
  Daryl Morey's and Luke Bornn's objections) alongside cases where they
  can still be used carefully; source for this module's "reading a
  profile honestly" discussion.

All player-level and team-level numbers cited in this module's notes,
lab, and slides (per-36 rates, z-scores, weighted coefficients of
variation, and the assists/turnovers/wins correlations) were computed
directly from the real data described above and can be reproduced by
running `Coding_Exercise/module3_visualizing_profiles.R`.
