# Module 2 Practice Questions (Ungraded)

These are for your own practice and do not count toward your grade. Try
each one before checking your work in RStudio.

1. In your own words, what does "pace" measure, and why do you need both
   a team's box score and its opponents' box score to compute it?

2. A team scores 108 points per game. Is that a good offense? What
   additional number would you need to answer that with confidence?

3. List Dean Oliver's Four Factors in the order of his original assigned
   weights (highest to lowest), and give one-sentence definition of each.

4. Effective field goal percentage (eFG%) treats a made three-pointer as
   worth more than a made two-pointer. Why does that matter for comparing
   two teams with very different three-point attempt rates?

5. In the 2017-18 data, effective field goal percentage correlates with
   wins at about 0.77, while net rating correlates with wins at about
   0.96. Explain, without just repeating the numbers, why net rating
   captures more of what makes a team win.

6. A team has a high offensive rebound rate but also a high turnover
   rate. What does that combination suggest about how that team plays,
   and why might the two factors partly cancel each other out?

7. Using `FF` from Lab 2, find the team with the best defensive rating
   (DRtg). Is it also a top-5 team in wins? What might explain a
   mismatch, if there is one?

8. Free throw rate had the second-strongest correlation with wins among
   the four offensive factors in this season's data (0.40), well behind
   eFG% (0.77) but ahead of offensive rebound rate (0.25) and turnover
   rate (-0.14). Propose one basketball explanation for why getting to
   the free throw line often would help a team win, beyond the free
   points themselves.
