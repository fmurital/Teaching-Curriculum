# Module 2 References

- Oliver, D. (2004). *Basketball on Paper: Rules and Tools for Performance
  Analysis*. Potomac Books (distributed by the University of Nebraska
  Press). ISBN 978-1-57488-688-7. Source of the Four Factors framework
  (effective field goal percentage, turnover rate, rebound rate, free
  throw rate) and Oliver's original 40/25/20/15 weighting.

- Zuccolotto, P., & Manisera, M. (2020). *Basketball Data Science: With
  Applications in R*, Chapter 2 ("Data and Basic Statistical Analyses").
  Chapman & Hall/CRC Data Science Series, CRC Press. Course textbook;
  this module maps to the first half of Chapter 2.

- Zuccolotto, P., & Manisera, M. `BasketballAnalyzeR` (R package). The
  `fourfactors()` function and its documentation are the direct source of
  this module's pace, rating, and Four Factors computations, run on the
  package's bundled real 2017-18 NBA season data (`Tbox`, `Obox`).

- Wikipedia contributors. "Three-point revolution." *Wikipedia, The Free
  Encyclopedia*. Used for background context on how NBA offensive pace
  and shot selection shifted over the 2010s (three-point attempts per
  game rose from about 2.8 after the line's 1979 introduction to about
  32.0 by the 2018-19 season).

All team-level numbers cited in this module's notes, lab, and slides
(pace, ratings, Four Factors, and their correlations with wins) were
computed directly from the real data described above and can be
reproduced by running `Coding_Exercise/module2_four_factors.R`.
