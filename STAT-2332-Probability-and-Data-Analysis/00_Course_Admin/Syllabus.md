## Course Description

This course is a foundational, calculus-based introduction to statistics
and probability. The following conceptual themes are developed through
the process of statistical investigation: exploratory data analysis
(univariate and bivariate), fundamentals of experiment design and
sampling, planning and conducting a study, exploring random phenomena
using probability and simulation, and the fundamentals of statistical
inference. Technology is integrated into each theme, and the statistical
software package used is chosen by the instructor.

## Topics Covered

Carried from the course's established coverage, to be confirmed against
the coordinator's Spring 2027 syllabus:

- Exploratory data analysis, univariate and bivariate
- Experiment design and sampling methods
- Planning and conducting a statistical study
- Probability and simulation for random phenomena
- Basic probability, complements, the addition rule, conditional
  probability
- Discrete distributions: binomial and Poisson
- The normal distribution and its applications
- The Central Limit Theorem for means and proportions
- Confidence intervals for means and proportions
- Hypothesis testing for means and proportions, including small samples
- Two-variable hypothesis testing
