# Module 04 Videos

- **"Ken Pomeroy Explains KenPom Rankings and Smarter March Madness
  Bracket Picks"** (YouTube: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qaMPOl9FZxI).
  A real concept-explainer, from the creator of one of the best-known
  opponent-adjusted rating systems in basketball, covering how
  strength-of-schedule adjustment works and why a raw rating can
  mislead without it. This is a basketball-specific explainer, not a
  generic sports one, and grounds this module's own opponent-adjusted
  `AdjRtg()` formula in the same real idea before we build it in code.
  Exact channel branding and runtime were not independently confirmed
  at the time this was added; verify before assigning, and swap in an
  alternative if the video has since been removed or re-titled.

- **"Warriors vs Cavaliers Christmas Day Full Highlights | Dec 25,
  2017"** (YouTube: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jxb7RhLlp4w). Real
  game footage from the real Christmas Day 2017 game in this module's
  own dataset (game number 10 in `module4_gsw_season_log.csv`), which
  Golden State really won 99-92, giving students real footage of the
  exact game whose real box-score numbers they compute in the lab.
  Exact channel name and runtime were not independently confirmed at
  the time this was added; verify before assigning.

No coding-tutorial video is assigned for this module, in keeping with
the graduate course's video-engagement standard: this is not a coding
class, and live in-class coding covers the R side directly. The videos
above are a real concept explainer and real game footage only.
