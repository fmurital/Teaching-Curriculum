# Module 3 Quiz (enter manually into D2L)

8 questions. Format notes for whoever builds this in D2L are in brackets.

**Q1.** [Multiple choice] This module's clutch-situation definition
requires:
A. Fewer than 10 minutes remaining and any score difference
B. Fewer than 5 minutes remaining (including overtime) and a score
   difference of 5 points or less
C. Any point in the fourth quarter only
D. A score difference of exactly 0
**Correct answer: B**

**Q2.** [Multiple choice] The Gini coefficient used in this module's
scoring inequality analysis is:
A. A measure of shooting accuracy
B. A measure of how concentrated scoring is among a team's players,
   originally developed to measure wealth concentration
C. Only defined for exactly five players
D. A measure of pace
**Correct answer: B**

**Q3.** [Multiple choice] In this module's real data, Boston Celtics'
scoring Gini coefficient in the clutch, compared to their general
split, was:
A. Lower (less concentrated)
B. Identical
C. Higher (more concentrated)
D. Not computable
**Correct answer: C**

**Q4.** [Multiple choice] In the player-level clutch indices, `Diff` is
calculated as:
A. `MINc - MIN`
B. `SPc - SP`
C. `AS - ASc`
D. `Prc - MINcp`
**Correct answer: B**

**Q5.** [Multiple choice] Goldman & Rao's (2012) MIT Sloan Sports
Analytics Conference paper argues that pressure affects NBA players:
A. Identically across all players, always reducing performance
B. Asymmetrically -- some respond with more effort, others with reduced
   shooting efficiency
C. Only during the regular season, not the playoffs
D. Only on defense, never on offense
**Correct answer: B**

**Q6.** [Numeric / short answer] What was Golden State's real defensive
rating (DRtg) in clutch situations in this module's 2018 playoff sample?
(Accept 113-117.)
**Correct answer: approximately 115.4**

**Q7.** [Numeric / short answer] What was Kyrie Irving's real `Pr_c`
(propensity to shoot in the clutch) in this module's Boston sample?
(Accept 180-200.)
**Correct answer: approximately 189.8**

**Q8.** [Short answer] In one or two sentences, explain why this module
uses real 2018 playoff data instead of the textbook's own 2021-22
Celtics/Warriors dataset, and name one real consequence that
substitution had on the analysis.
**Sample correct answer: The textbook's specific play-by-play file is
private and not part of the public BasketballAnalyzeR package, so this
module reproduces the same PbPmanipulation/TOPboxes/fourfactors/
inequality methodology on the real, publicly available 2018 playoff
data instead; one real consequence is a smaller sample (one postseason
rather than a full regular season plus playoffs), which meant far fewer
Boston players cleared the minimum clutch shot-attempt threshold than
would clear it in a full-season sample.**
