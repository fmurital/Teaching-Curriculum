# Injury Risk: Signature Module (PhD Track)

**Scheduled:** Week 10
**Level:** Graduate
**Textbook:** Zuccolotto, P., Manisera, M., & Sandri, M. (2026). *Advanced Basketball Data Science: With Applications in R*. CRC Press / Chapman & Hall.

## Overview

The instructor's own published research on injury detection and data quality in sport science, and the module where students identify a real injury-risk indicator in data and connect it to a specific model. This is the module for students who want to take on a large independent project.
