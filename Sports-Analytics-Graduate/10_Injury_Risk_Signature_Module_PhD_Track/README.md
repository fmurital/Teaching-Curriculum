# Module 10: Injury Risk Signature Module (PhD Track)

**Maps to:** Not from the course textbook -- built from the instructor's own published research program: Brown, Muritala & Haller (2026, *Research in Sports Medicine*, MLB pitcher reinjury) and Tention, Muritala, Frankel & Brown (2026, *Basketball Studies*, NBA player subsequent injury risk).

## Overview

A statistical process control approach to injury-risk monitoring, taught through two real, published papers in sequence: the original MLB pitcher reinjury method (a process-capability and CUSUM hybrid on game-by-game WHIP, with real Wainwright and Alzolay 2021-2023 case studies) and its basketball extension published this year (an individualized, credibility-weighted baseline with an EWMA-CUSUM hybrid statistic, evaluated on real PIE, Usage Rate, BPM, and Net Rating data across the 2021-2022 through 2025-2026 NBA seasons). The module closes with a live, real, freshly computed illustration on Victor Wembanyama's 2025-26 season and a direct discussion of open research directions for students considering a thesis, dissertation, or publication in this space.
