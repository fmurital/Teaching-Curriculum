# Quality Monitoring & Process Control in Sports (signature module)

**Scheduled:** Weeks 10-11
**Level:** Graduate

## Overview

Control charts and process capability (Cp) applied to athlete performance and injury data, built from Faruk Muritala's own published methodology (Muritala, Brown & Haller, 2026, Research in Sports Medicine) and the parallel West et al. (2026, JSCR) paper, taught in explicit contrast with the acute:chronic workload ratio literature and its documented statistical weaknesses (Kanwal et al., 2025, and related critiques).
