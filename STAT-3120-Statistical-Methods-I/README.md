# STAT 3120: Statistical Methods I

Spring 2027, Section 01, CRN 16519. Tuesdays and Thursdays, 2:20 PM to
3:40 PM, Atrium Building Room 2116, Marietta Campus. January 11 to
May 3, 2027. Enrollment cap 35.

Kennesaw State University, College of Computing and Software
Engineering, School of Data Science and Analytics.
Instructor: Faruk Muritala.

**Catalog description (verified against the current KSU catalog):**
foundation in statistical methods, including confidence intervals for
population parameters, correlation, simple linear regression, and
hypothesis testing (F and T-tests for regression, chi-square for
independence, 2 group and paired sample T-tests), taught with heavy
emphasis on statistical coding software and real-world datasets.
**Prerequisite:** STAT 1401 or DATA 1501 or STAT 2332 or MATH 3332.
