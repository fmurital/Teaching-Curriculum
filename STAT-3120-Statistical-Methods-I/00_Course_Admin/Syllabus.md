## Course Description

This course is designed to provide students with a foundation in
statistical methods, including confidence intervals for population
parameters, correlation, simple linear regression and hypothesis
testing (F and T-tests for regression, chi-square for independence, 2
group and paired sample T-tests). These concepts are taught with heavy
emphasis on statistical coding software and real-world datasets from a
variety of disciplines. (Verified against the current KSU catalog.)
