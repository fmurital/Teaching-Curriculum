# Module 1 Videos

## Posit Cloud Tutorial: How to Get Started with R and RStudio in Your Browser

Creator/Source: Alex Chisholm, product manager for Posit Cloud; published by Posit (the company behind RStudio) on its official open-source resources site.
Where to find it: https://opensource.posit.co/resources/videos/2023-07-27_posit-cloud-tutorial-how-to-get-started-with-r-and-rstudio-in-your-browser/
Duration: 28 minutes (July 27, 2023)
Learning Objective: Give students who would rather not install R and RStudio locally a verified, official walkthrough of Posit Cloud as an alternative, directly from the company that builds the tool.
Placement in Lecture: Segment A, as an optional preview linked from D2L for students considering Posit Cloud, rather than played live in class.
Discussion Questions: What are the tradeoffs between installing R and RStudio locally versus using Posit Cloud in a browser? Which would you choose for this course, and why?

No additional video content is assigned for Module 1. Later modules will link specific, verified resources where they are a genuine fit for that module's content, rather than added here for their own sake.
