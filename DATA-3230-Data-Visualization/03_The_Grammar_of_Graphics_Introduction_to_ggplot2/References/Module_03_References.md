# Module 3 References

## Required Reading

Course Student Notes (this module), covering the grammar of graphics, ggplot2's three required pieces, aesthetic mappings, and themes and scales as independent layers.

## Primary Framework Source

Wickham, H. (2010). A Layered Grammar of Graphics. *Journal of Computational and Graphical Statistics*, 19(1), 3-28. (The academic source for the layered grammar that `ggplot2` itself implements, building on Leland Wilkinson's original *The Grammar of Graphics* (2005, Springer).)

## Package Documentation

Wickham, H., Navarro, D., & Pedersen, T. L. (2024). *ggplot2: Elegant Graphics for Data Analysis* (3rd ed.). Springer. Available online at https://ggplot2-book.org/.

## Dataset Sources

De Cock, D. (2011). Ames, Iowa: Alternative to the Boston Housing Data as an End of Semester Regression Project. *Journal of Statistics Education*, 19(3).

`mpg`, the built-in dataset used for this module's single opening example, ships with the `ggplot2` package itself (fuel economy data for 38 popular car models, 1999-2008, originally from the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency).
