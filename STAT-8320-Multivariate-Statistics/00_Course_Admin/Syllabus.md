## Course Description

Survey course in statistical analysis techniques. Through a
combination of textbook and real-world data sets, students gain
hands-on experience in understanding when and how to utilize the
primary multivariate methods: data reduction techniques, including
Principal Components Analysis and Common Factor Analysis,
ANOVA/MANOVA/MANCOVA, Cluster Analysis, Survival Analysis, and
Decision Trees. (Verified against the current KSU catalog.) Identified
in the Spring 2027 course assignment email as the online section of
